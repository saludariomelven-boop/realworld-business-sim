MISSION

Compare assistant claims against actual visible game state and the controls/formulas shown in the live build. Do not guess when a value, mechanic, or current state cannot be verified.

A. RELEASE / FIRST-PAINT / BRANDING
1. Hard-refresh the public game and record its release/build evidence from the visible interface and script requests. No development/build labels should be visible to players.
2. The assistant's visible name must be “Business Partner.” The UI must not call it “AI,” show model/provider names, show provider connection/sign-in controls, show development phase numbers, or prompt the player to buy a personal provider quota.
3. Confirm the interface is usable on a narrow Android/mobile viewport and desktop, including open/close controls, keyboard submission, visible focus, scroll area, and error states.
4. Confirm normal game loading does not depend on the online assistant being available.

B. SHARED SERVICE / NO PERSONAL PAY-TO-PLAY FLOW
1. Confirm the app sends requests to the configured HTTPS shared-service endpoint and the Worker reports configured status. Never expose or copy its secret.
2. Test normal request/response and an ordinary multi-turn follow-up. Confirm no player provider sign-in, personal API key, or payment workflow appears.
3. Confirm quota/rate-limit/service-down errors are understandable and do not ask the player to pay. If the service is down, report exactly that; do not mark AI-dependent cases passed.
4. Check allowed-origin behavior, malformed/oversized messages, rate limiting, model fallback handling, and that secret values are never returned in errors/logged to the UI. A browser CORS test alone is not proof against abuse; report additional abuse-risk observations.
5. Check the Connection and privacy disclosure. It should explain that prompts go to Google Gemini through a shared service, that active-company context is sent when enabled, that free-tier provider data terms may allow prompts to improve products, and that the game owner may be billed if paid API billing is enabled.

C. COMPANY-STATE ACCURACY
Use a disposable company with known values and compare the assistant's answer with the actual current app. Test questions about:
- Company name, current week/status, cash and recent revenue/profit/history.
- Product prices, stock, demand, stock-outs, costs, and services if shown.
- Supplier orders, quantities, ETA/deliveries/logistics jobs, and events.
- Employees, wages/payroll obligations, loans/arrears, assets, liabilities, and recorded journal/history.
- Cash runway, risks, priority queue, market strategy, and saved decision log.

Ask the assistant to calculate at least one metric from recorded values. Check arithmetic, units, currency, use of missing/unknown data, and whether it clearly distinguishes real-world business principles from simulation mechanics. It must not fabricate zero values, rates, hidden rules, or current legal/tax facts. When context is toggled off, verify it does not receive the company snapshot; when on, verify it uses the right active company and never mixes data across companies.

D. GAME MECHANICS / ACTIONS / COMPANY MANAGEMENT
Ask specific practical prompts such as “What is causing my cash risk?”, “What should I prioritize this week and why?”, “Open finance,” “Show my stock report,” “Help me test a price change for [an actual goods product],” “Stage the premium market strategy,” “Prepare a sensible weekly marketing scenario,” and “Help me evaluate capacity investment.”

Verify that:
- Navigation opens the requested supported view, and read-only reports show the actual local company data.
- Price actions choose an exact existing goods item and one supported percentage, then fill the existing price field only after the player clicks “Stage for review.”
- Marketing scenarios use current budget and supported factors and only prefill the native budget form.
- Market positioning and capacity growth use the existing Layer 11 growth/strategy planners and their native cash/risk checks.
- The app communicates clearly what was staged and what still requires native saving/confirmation.
- Unsupported actions, invented products/IDs, unsupported percentages, malformed tool arguments, wrong-company state, or closed/bankrupt company changes are rejected safely.
- Asking for a loan, purchase, supplier order, hire/fire, payroll, week close, company deletion, or business closure results in accurate instructions and a link/route to relevant native controls, never a claim that the transaction already happened. In particular do not accidentally execute a week close or closure during QA.

Also ask “How does [a mechanic actually present in this build] work?” and “What should I do if [a known in-game problem] happens?” Verify the explanations from real screens/data/source behavior; if the game does not expose a rule, the assistant should clearly say it cannot verify that rule rather than inventing one.

F. ACCESSIBILITY / UX / ERROR HANDLING
Check 320–390 px mobile widths, tablet and desktop; tap targets; focus states; screen-reader labels; long responses; large company snapshots; message history growth; clear conversation; no response; malformed response; offline network; Worker 429 quota state; primary model failure with fallback; and both models unavailable. No transaction must silently execute because of a chat response.
